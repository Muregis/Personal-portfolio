About Me

From Curiosity to Code

I'm a Computer Science student at Kiambu National Polytechnic who discovered programming as a way to solve real problems I saw around me.

Growing up in Kenya, I noticed how many businesses struggled with outdated systems or couldn't afford expensive software. That frustration became my motivation — to build modern, affordable tech solutions specifically for African businesses.

At 20 years old, I've already built 5 enterprise products used by schools, Saccos, and businesses across Kenya. My stack is JavaScript-focused: React for beautiful interfaces, Node.js for powerful backends, PostgreSQL for reliable data, and the M-Pesa Daraja API for seamless payments, alongside proficiency in Java, Python, and C++.
